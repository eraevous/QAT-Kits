### ChatGPT said:

Briefly: the wallpaper works as a mirror for the narrator's confinement. She is prescribed total rest by her physician husband, kept in a room she didn't choose, and forbidden to write. The paper's tangled pattern and the figure she begins to see behind it track her own sense of being trapped inside a structure she can't read or escape. Many readers take the woman behind the pattern as the narrator herself, and the ending, where she tears the paper down and creeps around the room, as both a breakdown and a grim kind of liberation.
