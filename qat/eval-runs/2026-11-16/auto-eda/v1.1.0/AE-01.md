What stands out, read from the JSON summary:

- **`amount` is missing in 22.2% of rows** (111 of 500). It is the only column with gaps, so the missingness chart is all about it.
- **`amount` is strongly right-skewed** (skew 4.07): median 18.28, mean 29.28, max 355.90. A few large orders pull the mean up, so use the median or a log scale.
- **Returns are rare**: `is_return` is "no" in 92.4% of rows and "yes" in 7.6% (38 orders).
- **`constant_flag` has one value ("A") in every row**, so it carries no information. `amount` and `qty` are essentially uncorrelated (r = 0.05).

Also: `region` is fairly even (W 28.4%, E 26.6%, N 23.0%, S 22.0%), `order_id` is a unique ID (profiled, not charted), and `ordered_at` covers 2026-01-01 00:00 to 2026-01-21 19:00 at hourly steps.
