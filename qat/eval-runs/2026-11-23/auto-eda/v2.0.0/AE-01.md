Here's the EDA report for orders.csv (500 rows × 7 columns, 8 charts). What stands out:

- **`amount` is 22.2% missing** (111 of 500 rows). No other column has gaps.
- **`amount` is strongly right-skewed** (skew 4.07). The median is 18.28 and the mean is 29.28, with most orders between 9.58 and 34.53 (IQR) and a long tail up to 355.90. Use the median, or log it, when you summarise or model it.
- **Returns are rare.** `is_return` is "no" in 92.4% of rows and "yes" in 7.6% (38 orders). `region` is fairly even, from W at 28.4% down to S at 22.0%.
- **`constant_flag` is always "A"**, so it tells you nothing and can be dropped. `amount` and `qty` are essentially uncorrelated (r = 0.05). `ordered_at` is hourly from 2026-01-01 to 2026-01-21, so its rows-per-day line is flat by construction.

`order_id` is unique per row and is profiled but not charted.

**Steps taken**
1. `mkdir -p <run dir> && cd <run dir>`
2. `python3 gen_orders.py` (the snippet provided, which writes orders.csv)
3. `cp -r <skill_dir>/scripts ./auto_eda_scripts`
4. `python auto_eda_scripts/auto_eda.py orders.csv --out eda_out`
5. Read `eda_out/orders_eda.json` (kinds, per-column stats, correlations) for the findings above.
