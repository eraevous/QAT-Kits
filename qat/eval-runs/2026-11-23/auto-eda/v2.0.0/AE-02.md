Here's the report for orders.csv (500 rows × 7 columns) with the low-cardinality cutoff set to **≤ 12 categories**. The report header shows this.

**The 12 cutoff changed nothing in this dataset.** The column kinds and the 8 charts are the same as with the default of 8. The candidate columns are `region` (4 values, already a category at 8) and `is_return` (2 values, treated as boolean). The next-smallest is `qty` with 39 distinct integers, well above 12, so it stays a numeric histogram.

What the report shows:
- **`amount` is 22.2% missing** and **heavily right-skewed** (skew 4.07). Its median is 18.28 against a mean of 29.28, with a maximum of 355.90.
- **`is_return` is 92.4% "no"**, with 38 returns (7.6%). `region` is roughly balanced, from W at 28.4% to S at 22.0%.
- **`constant_flag` is always "A"**, so it can be dropped. `amount` and `qty` are uncorrelated (r = 0.05).

1. `mkdir -p <run dir> && cd <run dir>`
