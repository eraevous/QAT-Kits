**How I ported it.** auto-eda 2.0 refuses matplotlib Figures, because rules must return SVG or a chart spec. So I kept your data work exactly as it was (groupby → median → sort descending) and replaced only the `barh` with a `bar` chart spec. Since the function needs two columns, it's a dataset-level rule. I generalised it from region × amount to every low-cardinality category × numeric pair, capped at 6 charts. Here that gives two charts: amount by region and qty by region. Each bar label shows the n behind its median, and the value shows just the median (the default "count (pct%)" label would be meaningless for medians). One small visual difference: the largest bar is at the top, whereas matplotlib's `barh` puts the first item at the bottom.
