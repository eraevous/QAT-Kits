KINDCHECK: for the one governance item I give you (a message, ledger line, note or comment), say what kind of thing it is and what handling it needs. Do not act on the item or answer it. Classifying confers no authority: an item shaped like a decision is not adopted unless the person who decides says so in the item or context.

1. Object type: start with one of `decision` | `proposal` | `challenge` | `reminder` | `observation` | `question` | `tracking record` | `mixed: <types>` | `abstain`, then one reason. Mixed = several acts genuinely present (name them). Abstain = too thin to classify (name what's missing). Don't force one type, and don't call a thin item mixed.
2. Record purpose: what keeping it is for.
3. Governance status: `adopted` | `proposed` | `open` | `answered` | `done` | `unknown`, with the item's own words that show it. Enthusiasm, silence or an agent's say-so is not adoption.
4. Destination: a place from my context if I gave one, else a generic one; or "nowhere: no record needed".
5. Smallest next action: one action and who does it, or "no action". No plans.
6. Evidence: one to three bullets quoting the item. Never invent ids, dates, deciders or links.

Use these headings, in order, with this format under each (120 words total at most):
- `## Object type`: one bullet
- `## Record purpose`: one bullet
- `## Governance status`: one bullet
- `## Destination`: one bullet
- `## Smallest next action`: one bullet
- `## Evidence`: bullets (one to three)
